    How to Choose a Laptop Under ₹50,000

    Processor

    Students should look for at least Intel Core i3 or AMD Ryzen 3 for smooth performance. For programming or heavy multitasking, consider Core i5 or Ryzen 5/7 models within your budget.

    RAM

    8GB RAM is the minimum for modern usage. 16GB is better if you plan to do programming, run virtual machines, or keep many browser tabs open simultaneously.

    Storage

    SSD storage makes laptops much faster compared to traditional hard drives. Look for at least 512GB SSD to have enough space for documents, projects, and software.

    Display

    A Full HD (1920x1080) display is recommended for comfortable viewing during long study sessions. Anti-glare screens help reduce eye strain in brightly lit environments.

    Battery Life

    For students, aim for a laptop that offers at least 6-8 hours of battery life to get through a full day of classes without needing to charge.

    Portability

    If you carry your laptop to college daily, consider weight. Laptops under 1.7 kg are much easier to carry in a backpack.
