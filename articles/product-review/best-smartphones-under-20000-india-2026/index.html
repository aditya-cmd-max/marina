    7. Final Verdict: Which One Should You Buy?

    Choosing the right phone depends on what you value most. After spending hours comparing these devices, here's my simple advice:

    • If you're a gamer or want the best all-rounder: Go for the realme NARZO 80 Pro 5G. It's fast, has a great display, and the IP69 rating is a bonus.
